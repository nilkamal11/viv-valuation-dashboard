Fund constituent links retain dated primary-manager research. Structured-note references also remain nonadditive because basket weights and payoff sensitivities are not supplied.

The replacement statement reports monthly returns for 2026 through 7 September but no annual history, holding-level attribution, or strategy benchmark.
